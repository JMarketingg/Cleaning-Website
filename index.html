How it will work

Once TidyHaus launches, we plan for the process to work like this. None of these steps are available yet.

  1. Share your requirements

    You'll tell us your area, property type and size, and preferred date.

  2. Review your service estimate

    We'll review the details and prepare an estimate for you.

  3. Confirm your booking

    If you accept the estimate, we'll confirm the date and what's included.

  4. Receive your appointment details

    We plan to assign a cleaner and send you the appointment details.

  5. Cleaning and quality check

    The agreed tasks are completed, then checked against our cleaning checklist.

  6. Share your feedback

    We'll ask how the cleaning went so we can keep improving.
