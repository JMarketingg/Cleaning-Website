We haven't set prices yet. It'll depend on how big your place is, which service you pick and the date.

Can you serve my area?

Makati and BGC to start. If you're somewhere else, message us anyway so we know.

Do you clean rental or short-stay units?

Not at first. We're starting with regular home and condo cleaning.

Will cleaners bring their own supplies?

It depends on the job. We'll tell you in your quote.

Do I need to be home, and what about condo building rules?

We're still working out building passes and keys. We'll sort it out with you before your first clean.

How will I pay?

We'll let you know how to pay before you book.

What do you need from me for a quote?

Your city, what kind of place it is, roughly how big, which service and what date. Please don't send us door codes or building access info.

What do you do with my info?

This site doesn't collect any. Our privacy page has more.
