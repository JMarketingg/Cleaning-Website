Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve Makati and Fort Bonifacio (BGC) in Metro Manila.

How do I book a cleaning?

Fill out the quote request form below with your details and the service you need. We'll get back to you to confirm scheduling and pricing.

Yes — weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly subscription cleaning is available at a discounted rate compared to one-time bookings.

Dusting, sweeping/mopping, surface disinfection, bathroom and kitchen cleaning, and trash removal. Deep cleaning and add-ons like organization can be requested separately.

No, our team brings all necessary cleaning supplies and equipment.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the size of the space, type of service, and frequency. Request a free quote and we'll give you a clear estimate before booking.
