How it works

From request to clean home in six steps

How booking will work once we launch.

  1. Share your requirements

    Tell us your area, property type and size, and preferred date.

  2. Get your estimate

    We review the details and prepare an estimate for you.

  3. Confirm your booking

    Accept the estimate and we'll confirm the date and what's included.

  4. Receive appointment details

    We assign a cleaner and send you the appointment details.

  5. Cleaning and quality check

    The agreed tasks are completed, then checked against our checklist.

  6. Share your feedback

    Tell us how it went so we can keep improving.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you taking bookings now?

Not yet. TidyHaus is preparing to launch and is not accepting inquiries or bookings at this stage.

How much does cleaning cost?

Prices haven't been set yet. We plan to base them on the size of your space, the service, and the date.

Can you serve my area?

We plan to start with Makati and Taguig (BGC). Coverage hasn't been confirmed yet.

Do you clean rental or short-stay units?

Our first planned services are standard home cleaning and condo cleaning. Rental turnover cleaning is not planned for the initial launch.

Will cleaners bring their own supplies?

Supply arrangements are decided case by case and will be confirmed in each quotation.

Do I need to be home, and what about condo building rules?

Access arrangements, such as building passes and key handover, haven't been finalized yet. We'll share details before launch.

How will I pay?

Payment methods haven't been finalized yet. Any payment options will be confirmed before a booking is made.

What will you ask for when I request an estimate?

Your area, property type and approximate size, the service you're interested in, your preferred date, and how you'd like us to contact you. Please don't share door codes or building access details through the website.

How do you handle my information?

This website doesn't collect any personal information. A draft Privacy Notice is a placeholder until inquiries open.

Want to know when we launch?

Follow along or send us a question on Facebook. Bookings aren't open yet.
