Cleaning in BGC condos

We work around your building

Every BGC tower has its own rules for cleaners and service providers. Here's how we handle them, so you don't have to.

  • Admin clearance and passesTell us your building. If the admin needs our cleaners' names or IDs ahead of time, we'll send them before your booking.
  • Service elevators and allowed hoursSome buildings only allow service work at set times. We'll pick a schedule that fits your building's rules.
  • Keys and accessFor safety, please give door codes in person or leave them with your building's guard or reception, not by message.
  • Short-stay unitsMany BGC buildings have their own rules for short-stay rentals. We'll plan turnovers around your building's rules and your guests' check-in times.

How it works

How booking works

From booking to a clean home in six easy steps. Most people finish booking in about 2 minutes.

  1. See your price

    Answer a few questions on our booking form: your area, the service and the size of your place. Your price shows up right away.

  2. Pick an open date

    Choose a date and start time that's still open. Taken times are greyed out, so no double bookings.

    Choose GCash, Maya or credit/debit card, then submit. We don't take cash.

    We email your booking confirmation and payment details, then sort out building access with you.

  5. We clean

    We follow the room-by-room checklist, then go over it again before we leave.

  6. Tell us how we did

    Good or bad, we really want to hear it. It's how we get better.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a cleaning in BGC?

Just use our online booking form. It takes about 2 minutes. You'll see your price, pick an open date and time, and choose how to pay. We'll then email your booking confirmation.

Can you work with my building's admin rules?

Yes. Tell us your building and we'll prepare what the admin needs, like cleaner names or IDs, and book a time that fits the building's service hours.

Do you clean Airbnb and short-stay units in BGC?

Yes. Just make sure your building allows short-stay rentals. Pick "Airbnb & Rental" on the booking form to see your price.

Do you clean offices in BGC?

Yes, small offices and workspaces. Pick "Office" on the booking form and we'll send your price with your confirmation.

Do you clean in other parts of Taguig?

We focus on BGC and Makati. If you're elsewhere in Taguig, message us on Facebook and we'll let you know.

What if you miss something?

Tell us within 24 hours of your clean. We'll come back and fix anything we missed on the checklist, at no extra cost.

How much does condo cleaning cost in BGC?

A standard clean starts at ₱1,300 for a studio, and a deep clean starts at ₱1,900. Your exact price depends on the service, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and how often you book. Weekly bookings get 15% off, every 2 weeks 10% off, and monthly 5% off. The booking form shows your price before you submit.

Will cleaners bring their own supplies?

It depends on the job. If you have a question about supplies, message us before your clean and we'll let you know.

What do you do with my info?

We only use your details to handle your booking, and we count website visits to improve the site. Our privacy notice explains everything.
