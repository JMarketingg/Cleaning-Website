Hiring cleaners in Makati & BGC

We're looking for cleaners for homes and condos in Makati and BGC (Taguig). You choose the jobs you take.

Pay

  • 55% of the price of every job, at least ₱650 per job
  • All tips are yours
  • Two cleaners on one job split the 55% and the tips
  • Add-ons (inside the fridge, oven or cabinets): 55% too
  • Extra time the customer approves (₱300 per hour per cleaner): 55% too
  • Travel fee for jobs outside Makati and BGC: all yours
  • Paid by GCash after every job

Our rules

  • Be on time, and message the customer before you arrive
  • Follow the TidyHaus checklist in the Cleaner App
  • Take before and after photos, with no faces or personal items
  • No cash: customers pay TidyHaus by GCash
  • Don't touch the customer's personal things
  • Be polite and respectful
  • If the place is much messier than normal, or used for storage or business, message TidyHaus before you start
  • Things that aren't included (curtains, outside windows, climbing ladders) are not done. Tell TidyHaus first.
  • If we missed something on the checklist and the customer tells us within 12 hours, you go back to fix it at no extra pay

If something gets broken

  • An accident you tell us about right away: no pay is taken off. TidyHaus handles it with the customer.
  • Hiding a broken item: you may not get more jobs.
  • Careless again and again: we talk first, and you may be let go if it keeps happening.
  • Stealing: you are let go right away, and the customer and building are told.

What you need

  • A valid ID
  • Your own cleaning equipment (mop, cloths, and a vacuum if you have one)
  • A phone that can open our Cleaner App
