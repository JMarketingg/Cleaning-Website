Terms of Service

Last updated: November 20, 2026

These terms explain how booking a cleaning with TidyHaus Home Cleaning ("TidyHaus", "we") works. By paying a deposit or booking a cleaning, you agree to them.

How booking works

  1. You send a request on our website or by message.
  2. We send you your price.
  3. You pay a deposit to reserve your date (see below). Your date is only held once the deposit is paid.
  4. We email or message your booking confirmation.
  5. You pay the rest on the day, before or after the cleaning.

Deposit and payment

  • A 25% deposit reserves your date. We round it up to the next ₱50. It counts toward your price.
  • If you book less than 24 hours before your cleaning, the full amount is paid to book.
  • We accept GCash, Maya and bank transfer (InstaPay / QR Ph). We don't accept cash.
  • Please put your booking reference in the payment note, and send us a screenshot of your payment.
  • Weekly and every-2-weeks customers pay the deposit for their first visit. Later visits are paid before each visit.

Cancellation policy

In short: pay 25% to reserve your date. Cancel or move 24+ hours before: free. Less than 24 hours before, or not home: the deposit is not refunded. If we cancel: full refund.

The details:

  • 24 hours or more before your cleaning: free to cancel. We refund your deposit or keep it as credit for a new date.
  • Changing your date: free if you tell us at least 24 hours before. You can move your date once for free. After that, a new deposit may be needed.
  • Less than 24 hours before, or no one home: the deposit is not refunded.
  • If our cleaner can't get in on the day (for example, no gate pass or building clearance), we'll talk with you and decide together. Please arrange gate passes before your cleaning.
  • If we have to cancel, you get your whole deposit back, or we move your cleaning to a date that works for you.

Rewards

  • Refer a friend: every customer has a referral code (it's in your booking confirmation). A new customer who books with your code gets ₱200 off their first cleaning. After their first cleaning is done, you get ₱100 off your next cleaning. It can't be combined with FIRSTCLEAN.
  • Every 10th cleaning: ₱500 off. Free re-cleans don't count.
  • Prepaid package: weekly or every-2-weeks customers can pay for 4 visits upfront and save 5% on top of their regular discount. Visits can be moved with 24 hours' notice.
  • Same cleaner: you can ask for the same cleaner as last time. We do our best, but it depends on their schedule.

Same-day booking

You can book for today until 12 noon, for a start time at least 2 hours away, if we have an open time. Same-day bookings are paid in full to book.

Your price

Our prices are for homes in normal, lived-in condition. If your place is very cluttered or also used for storage or business, please tell us so we can price it right. Each time slot includes a set number of hours for 1 cleaner. Going over them is ₱300 per hour per cleaner, and we always ask you first.

Our promise

If we miss anything on our checklist, tell us within 12 hours of your cleaning and we'll come back to fix it, free of charge.

Text messages

If you agree to texts, we may text you about your booking: confirmations, on-the-way updates, payment details, replies about your job, and one follow-up after your cleaning. Texts are optional and not needed to book. How often we text depends on your booking. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts, or HELP for help.
