Privacy Notice

This notice explains how TidyHaus House Cleaning Manila ("TidyHaus", "we") handles personal information you send us when you request a cleaning quote.

Who we are

TidyHaus House Cleaning Manila is a home and condo cleaning service preparing to launch in Metro Manila, Philippines. You can contact us at tidyhauscleaningph@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you request a quote, we ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your preferred contact method and contact details
  • Your general area (not your full address)
  • Property type and approximate size
  • The service you're interested in and your preferred date
  • Any notes you choose to add

Please don't send door codes, building access details, ID numbers, or payment information through the quote form or by email.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to respond to your quote request and, if you choose to book once bookings open, to arrange the service. We don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who can access it

Quote requests are received in our business email, which is provided by Google (Gmail). The website form is not yet connected; when it is, we expect to use a form-handling provider to deliver requests to that email, and this notice will be updated to name it. Your details may be shared with a cleaner only if you book, and only what they need to do the job.

How long we keep it

We propose to delete quote requests that don't lead to a booking within six (6) months. This retention period is a working proposal and will be reviewed before launch.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing tidyhauscleaningph@gmail.com. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you also have the right to raise concerns with the National Privacy Commission.

Changes to this notice

We'll update this page if how we handle information changes.
