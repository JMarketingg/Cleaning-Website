Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This notice explains how TidyHaus House Cleaning Manila ("TidyHaus", "we") handles personal information when you use this website or contact us for a cleaning quote.

Who we are

TidyHaus is a home, condo, Airbnb and office cleaning service in Makati and Bonifacio Global City (BGC), Taguig. Our office is at 21st Floor, 8 Rockwell Dr, Makati City, 1210 Metro Manila, Philippines.

What this website collects

This website does not have accounts, sign-ups, analytics or advertising trackers.

When you submit the quote request form, the details you enter are sent to us by email. If you give your email address, we also send you a confirmation email. Nothing is sent until you press "Submit request".

Outside services involved when you use this website:

  • Our website host processes standard technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver pages to your browser.
  • Resend, our email service, delivers quote requests to us and confirmation emails to you.
  • Our booking system (hosted with Vercel and Supabase) stores your booking request. It also counts website visits: the pages you view, how long you stay, the buttons you click, your device type and your approximate city. It uses a random ID saved in your browser, not your name or number.
  • Google Maps is embedded on some pages. Google may collect information such as your IP address and set cookies under its own privacy policy.

Information you send us

When you submit the quote form, or message us by text, Facebook Messenger, Instagram or TikTok, we receive what you choose to send. For a quote, this usually includes:

  • Your name, mobile number and, if you give it, your email address
  • Your area and building or street
  • The type and size of your place, the service you need, and your preferred date
  • Any notes you add

For safety, please don't send door codes, passwords or building access details by message. Give them in person or leave them with your building's guard or reception.

How we use it

We use your information only to:

  • Reply to you and send a quote
  • Schedule and carry out the cleaning you book
  • Contact you about your booking, including follow-ups such as our re-clean guarantee

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your information is seen by the TidyHaus team handling your request, and by the cleaners assigned to your booking, who receive only what they need for the job. Messages you send through Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or your mobile network are also handled by those companies under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to reply to you, provide the service you booked, and handle any follow-up. After that, we delete it, unless we need to keep a record for legal or tax reasons.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop using your information

You can also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

How to contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us by text or call at +63 992 309 5621, or message us on Facebook (opens in a new tab).

Changes to this notice

We'll update this page if the way we handle information changes, for example if we add new tools for bookings or text messages. The date at the top shows the latest version.
